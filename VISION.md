# WaxPrep Vision

## Mission
Help Nigerian students succeed in their exams through a personalized AI tutor on WhatsApp.

## Target Users
JSS, SS, and pre-university students preparing for WAEC, JAMB, NECO, and Post-UTME.

## Core Promise
Every student has a tutor that remembers them and adapts to their goals.

## Current Milestone
One student. One subject. One week. One remembered conversation.

## Out of Scope (For Now)
Payments, dashboards, gamification, multiple AI personas, advanced analytics, mobile apps, and any feature that doesn't directly help validate the core tutoring experience.

## The Rule
If a feature doesn't help achieve the current milestone, it goes into ideas.md instead of into the codebase.
